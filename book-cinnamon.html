Cinnamon and the Christmas Prayer Box

Children's Christmas Story · Faith · Family · Coming Soon

Even when loved ones are far away, prayer can keep hearts close together.

FaithFamilyChristmasHope

Christmas is just around the corner, but for Karlie, the season doesn't feel quite the same. With her father away serving in the Army, she misses him more than ever.

When a playful kitten named Cinnamon arrives and her grandmother introduces her to a special Christmas Prayer Box, Karlie discovers a new way to share her worries, hopes, and prayers with God. As snow falls and holiday traditions unfold, Karlie learns that even when loved ones are far away, faith, family, and prayer can keep hearts close together.

Perfect for families looking for a heartwarming Christmas story centered on faith and gratitude.
