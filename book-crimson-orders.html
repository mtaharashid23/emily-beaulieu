Crimson Orders

Crimson Orders

Released August 14th

Captain Kyson Iron Claw has spent years living two lives.

To the United States military, he's a decorated Special Operations captain trusted with the nation's most dangerous missions.

To the shifter world, he's known only as the Crimson King—the powerful Alpha who has sworn to protect every pack hidden across the country.

When mysterious attacks begin wiping out shifter communities, Dexter goes undercover to uncover the truth. Every clue leads him closer to a conspiracy hidden within the military itself, one determined to hunt his people into extinction.

Then he meets Lyra Harrow.

Quiet, kind, and carrying invisible scars, Valerie has spent years living under the control of a father who has convinced her she isn't strong enough to stand on her own. The day Dexter witnesses her fear, his wolf recognizes what his mind refuses to accept.

She's his mate.

Claiming her could expose the greatest secret the shifter world has ever protected. Walking away could leave her trapped in a life she never chose.

As enemies close in and old loyalties are tested, Dexter must decide where his duty truly lies—with the mission he was sent to complete... or the woman fate chose for him.

Because some orders are meant to be followed.

Others are worth starting a war to defy.
