The Healer at War

Christian Historical Romance

She carried herbs into battle… and found love in the middle of loss.

FaithSacrificeHealingResilience

As war sweeps across a divided nation, Ruth Talcott devotes her life to caring for the wounded with the herbal remedies and healing knowledge passed down through her family. When she crosses paths with Nathaniel, a young soldier carrying both physical and emotional scars, an unexpected bond begins to grow.

As battles rage and loss threatens everything they hold dear, Ruth must rely on her faith, courage, and determination to bring hope to those around her. Filled with love, sacrifice, healing, and God's guiding hand, The Healer at War is a moving story of resilience and the power of faith during life's darkest moments.
