Every story starts in the garden

By day, Emily teaches young children. By evening, she's homesteading with her mother — caring for animals, growing herbs, and learning the traditional remedies passed down through her family. Those quiet, hands-on hours became the soil for every book she writes.

Whether it's a mischievous kitten leading four friends into a hidden garden, or a Civil War nurse carrying lavender into a field hospital, Emily's stories are rooted in the belief that learning — and healing — happen outside the lines.
