The Herbalist Club, Book Two

Middle Grade Adventure · Ages 8–12

Almonds' chaos leads to a hidden wonder.

MysteryFriendshipOutdoor AdventureHerbal Learning

Summer camp at PineWhisper is supposed to be relaxing — but Almonds has other plans! When the fluffy gray kitten leads the group deep into the woods, the young herbalists discover a hidden garden filled with forgotten secrets.

As Eliza, Damon, Luna, and Athena uncover clues from the past, they learn more about herbs, friendship, and the wonders of the natural world. The garden was hers once. Now it's theirs to protect.
